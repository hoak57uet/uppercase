Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is an Ethereum (ETH) Wallet?

An Ethereum wallet is a digital storage address that allows you to hold, send, and receive Ether (ETH) and tokens on the Ethereum blockchain. It also works with other EVM-compatible networks, like Binance Smart Chain and Polygon.

2. What does EVM-Compatible mean?

EVM stands for Ethereum Virtual Machine. EVM-compatible wallets can be used on various blockchains that support Ethereum’s ecosystem, such as Binance Smart Chain, Avalanche, and Fantom. These wallets operate similarly to Ethereum wallets.

3. Is it safe to generate wallets online?

Yes, it is safe if the generator uses client-side scripts for wallet generation. This means that all private keys and wallet addresses are created directly in your browser without being sent to a server.

4. Why should I download my wallets in a CSV file?

Downloading wallets in a CSV file allows you to store multiple wallet addresses and private keys safely offline. It also provides an easy way to manage and backup your addresses for future use.

5. Can I create wallets for other networks, like Binance Smart Chain or Polygon?

Yes, our generator creates wallets compatible with Ethereum and any EVM-compatible network. This allows you to use your wallet addresses across networks like Binance Smart Chain, Polygon, Avalanche, and more.

6. What should I do if I lose my private key?

Unfortunately, if you lose your private key, you lose access to your funds in that wallet. Always keep a secure backup of your private keys in a safe location.

7. Can I use these wallets to store tokens?

Yes, Ethereum wallets can store ETH as well as ERC-20, ERC-721, and other token standards on the Ethereum network. On EVM-compatible networks, you can also store native assets and supported tokens.
